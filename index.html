GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Have a specific requirement? Contact us and we can discuss what may be feasible.

Ask a Question
Can I inquire about bulk orders?

Yes. Share the product, estimated quantity, destination country, and any packaging or specification requirements. Minimum order quantities and feasibility will need to be confirmed for the specific product.

Are all products shown currently available?

No. The categories on this website indicate areas of interest and exploration. Availability, supplier options, pricing, and lead times are not guaranteed and will be confirmed individually.

Can you discuss private-label or custom packaging?

You can submit a private-label or custom packaging inquiry. Feasibility depends on supplier capabilities, order volume, packaging specifications, labelling rules, and applicable regulations.

What is required to export food products to Dubai?

Requirements depend on the product and transaction and may include applicable Indian export documentation, UAE import approvals, product registration, labelling, food-safety, and shipment documents. Requirements should be verified with the relevant authorities and qualified trade or logistics professionals before an order is confirmed.

Can I request samples?

Yes, you may ask about samples. Availability, sample cost, shipping, and whether samples can be sent to your destination will depend on the product and supplier.

Which export documents can be provided?

Documentation depends on the product, origin, destination, transaction structure, and applicable rules. Specific documents and the ability to provide them must be confirmed before making any commitment.
