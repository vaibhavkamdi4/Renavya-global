Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Renavya Global intends to handle information submitted through this website. Please review and update this policy to reflect your final business practices and any applicable legal requirements before publishing.

Information we may collect

If you submit an inquiry, we may receive your name, business email address, company name, country, product interest, estimated quantity, and message. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through the contact form.

How information may be used

Inquiry information is used to review and respond to your business request, clarify product or sourcing requirements, and maintain relevant business correspondence. We do not claim to sell personal information.

Third-party services

The inquiry form is designed to use Formspree or a similar form-processing provider. If enabled, information you submit will be processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. The WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp, which is operated by a separate provider and is subject to its terms and privacy practices. Review the providers' policies before using these services.

Retention and security

Inquiry details should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, meet recordkeeping obligations, or resolve disputes. Appropriate access and security measures should be used. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

If you wish to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, contact the business using the email address published on the website. Replace the email placeholder with a monitored business address before launch.

Children

This website is intended for business inquiries and is not directed at children.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, services, and applicable requirements change. The date at the top should be updated when material changes are made.
